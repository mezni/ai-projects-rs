# AW-PLN-003 Prepaid Plans

## 1. Plans
1.1 Lite: 5 GB of data for $15 per month.
1.2 Plus: 20 GB of data for $30 per month.
1.3 Unlimited: unlimited data, throttled after 50 GB, for $50 per month.

## 2. Top-ups
2.1 Top-ups expire after 90 days.
2.2 Unused data does not roll over to the next month.
