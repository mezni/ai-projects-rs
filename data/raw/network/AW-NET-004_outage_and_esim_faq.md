# AW-NET-004 Outages and eSIM FAQ

## 1. Outages
1.1 Check the current network status in the Aether app.
1.2 Customers affected by an outage longer than 24 hours receive a credit equal to one day of their plan fee.

## 2. eSIM
2.1 Activate an eSIM by scanning the QR code in the Aether app.
2.2 Error 31 means the QR code has already been used. Request a new QR code in the app.
