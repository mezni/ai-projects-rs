# AW-BIL-001 Billing Dispute Policy

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to postpaid customers of Aether Wireless.

## 2. Filing a dispute
2.1 Customers may dispute a charge within 60 days of the invoice date.
2.2 Disputes are filed in the Aether app or by calling customer support.

## 3. Review
3.1 Aether acknowledges a dispute within 2 business days.
3.2 Disputed amounts are not subject to late fees while the dispute is under review.

## 4. Outcomes
4.1 If the dispute is upheld, a credit appears on the next invoice.
4.2 If the dispute is rejected, the customer receives a written explanation and may escalate to the Customer Resolution Team.
