# AW-ROA-002 Roaming Policy (v1)

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to postpaid and prepaid customers.

## 2. Zones
2.1 Zone A covers the EU, EEA and the UK. Your domestic allowance applies, with a fair-use limit of 10 GB per month.
2.2 Zone B covers Japan, Australia, the USA and Canada. Data is billed at $10 per GB.

## 3. Prepaid customers
3.1 Prepaid customers cannot use data roaming in Zone B.
