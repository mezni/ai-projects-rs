# AW-ROA-002 Roaming Policy (v2)

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to postpaid and prepaid customers.

## 2. Zones
2.1 Zone A covers the EU, EEA and the UK. Your domestic allowance applies, with a fair-use limit of 15 GB per month.
2.2 Zone B covers Japan, Australia, the USA and Canada. Data is billed at $8 per GB.

## 3. Prepaid customers
3.1 Prepaid customers can use data roaming in Zone B by buying a Roaming Pass.
3.2 The Roaming Pass costs $15 and includes 1 GB, valid for 7 days.
